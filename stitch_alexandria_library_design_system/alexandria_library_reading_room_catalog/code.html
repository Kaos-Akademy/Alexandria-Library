The Awakening · Chapter IV

Domestic Routines & Defiance

It was a strange feeling that overtook Edna Pontellier as the afternoon shadows lengthened across the wide veranda. The voice of the sea spoke directly to her soul in a murmur both seductive and unquiet—never ceasing, whispering of solitude, of wide spaces, of abysses untouched by domestic duty. It invited the soul to wander in abysses of solitude.
